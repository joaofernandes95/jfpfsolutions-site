Games

Eleven games, one phone

Pass the phone round: it deals the secret words, keeps the time and the scores, and knows whose turn it is.

  • Bluff and deduce

    Impostor: everyone sees the secret word except the impostor. Mafia: secret roles, and the phone reads the night out loud and calls each role, so nobody has to sit out as the narrator.

  • Guess the word

    On your forehead (tilt the phone when you guess), Forbidden words, Three rounds (describe, one word, mime) and Draw: on your own or in teams.

  • Quick and loud

    Bomb: say something that fits and pass the phone before it goes off. Stop: a letter, some categories, pens ready.

  • Get to know each other

    Most likely (on three, everyone points), Never have I ever (fingers down) and Truth or dare, with an optional spicy pack: cheeky, never explicit.

  • Thousands of words, from here

    Words, cards and questions in European Portuguese, with bifanas, Santos Populares, Queima das Fitas, football and novelas, and in English. They don’t come back until the whole deck has been played.

  • Your own words

    Make lists of your group’s names, places and inside jokes, and play them in any game.

  • Made for the table

    Players, teams, turns, timers and scores taken care of. Big, dark screens that can be read across the table, with sounds and haptics.

  • No ads, no internet

    No ads, no account, no tracking. It works on a plane, at the beach, or in a cellar with no signal.

FAQ

Questions

Does everyone need the app?

No. Malta is played on one iPhone that goes round the table, so only whoever hosts needs it, and only they need Malta Complete.

Não. O Malta joga-se num só iPhone que dá a volta à mesa, por isso só quem recebe a malta precisa da app, e só essa pessoa precisa da Malta Completa.

How many players?

Mafia needs 5 or more; the team games (Forbidden words, Three rounds, Draw) need 4; Impostor and Most likely need 3; the others work from 2, and Stop with any number. There’s no maximum, but past about a dozen the turns get long.

A Máfia precisa de 5 ou mais; os jogos de equipas (Palavras proibidas, Três rondas, Desenha) precisam de 4; o Impostor e o Quem é mais provável precisam de 3; os outros jogam-se a partir de 2, e o Stop com qualquer número. Não há máximo, mas a partir de uma dúzia as vezes demoram.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is on the phone, so it works anywhere: on a plane, at the beach, in a cellar with no signal.

Não. Está tudo no telemóvel, por isso funciona em qualquer sítio: num avião, na praia, numa cave sem rede.

In which languages?

The app follows your iPhone’s language, English or Portuguese. The words, cards and questions can be in European Portuguese or English whatever the iPhone’s language: change them in Settings or in each game.

A app segue a língua do iPhone, português ou inglês. As palavras, cartas e perguntas podem ser em português de Portugal ou em inglês, seja qual for a língua do iPhone: mude-as nas Definições ou em cada jogo.

How spicy are the spicy questions?

Cheeky, never explicit: dating, crushes, exes, first kisses and nights out. They’re off unless you turn them on, and no dare involves touching anyone. Anyone can refuse; Truth or dare just counts it.

Atrevidas, nunca explícitas: namoros, paixonetas, ex, primeiros beijos e noites fora. Estão desligadas até as ligar, e nenhuma consequência obriga a tocar em ninguém. Qualquer pessoa pode recusar; o Verdade ou consequência só conta.

Tilting doesn’t work in On your forehead

Hold the phone upright on your forehead, sideways, with the screen facing the others. Tilt it clearly forwards (guessed) or backwards (pass), then bring it back upright before the next one. If you’d rather tap, turn off “Tilt the phone to answer” in the game: then the right half of the screen is guessed and the left half is pass.

Segure o telemóvel direito na testa, deitado, com o ecrã virado para os outros. Incline-o bem para a frente (adivinhou) ou para trás (passar) e volte a pô-lo direito antes da seguinte. Se preferir tocar, desligue «Inclinar o telemóvel para responder» no jogo: aí, a metade direita do ecrã é adivinhou e a esquerda é passar.

I bought Malta Complete. How do I get it on another iPhone?

It belongs to your Apple Account, and your family has it too with Family Sharing. It usually appears by itself; if not, open Settings › Malta Complete › Restore purchase.

Fica associada à sua conta Apple, e a sua família também a tem com a Partilha com a família. Normalmente aparece sozinha; se não, abra Definições › Malta Completa › Restaurar compra.

Does Malta collect anything?

No. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us. The players’ names and your lists stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Não. Não há conta, anúncios nem rastreamento, e nada nos é enviado. Os nomes dos jogadores e as vossas listas ficam no seu iPhone. Consulte a política de privacidade.

How do I get help, or suggest words?

Email support@jfpfsolutions.com: problems, a word that’s wrong, or ones you’d like to see. We usually reply within one business day.

Envie um email para support@jfpfsolutions.com: problemas, uma palavra errada, ou palavras que gostava de ver. Normalmente respondemos num dia útil.
