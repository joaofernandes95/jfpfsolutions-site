About

Descriptions to copy

Use them as they are or cut them to fit.

In one sentence

Cuco is a visual timer for small children on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad: they watch the time run out from across the room, go through their routines one step at a time, and an animal pops out of a present when the time is up.

O Cuco é um temporizador visual para crianças pequenas na Apple TV, no iPhone e no iPad: veem o tempo a passar do outro lado da sala, fazem as rotinas um passo de cada vez, e quando o tempo acaba sai um animal de um presente.

In a paragraph

Small children can’t read a clock yet, but they can watch time run out. Cuco fills the living-room TV with the time left, big enough to see from the sofa: a shrinking disc, an hourglass, a draining jar, a little car driving home, or a picture that appears piece by piece. Morning, bedtime and tidy-up routines go one step at a time, each with its own timer and picture, and parents can use photos of the real toothbrush or school bag for children who can’t read. Like a cuckoo clock, when the time is up a present wiggles and one of sixteen animals pops out; find them all and Cuco the cuckoo appears. Children earn stars and badges, each child has their own profile, and with iCloud everything is the same on the Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. There are no ads, no tracking and no account.

As crianças pequenas ainda não sabem ler as horas, mas conseguem ver o tempo a passar. O Cuco mostra o tempo que falta na TV da sala, em grande, para se ver do sofá: um disco que vai diminuindo, uma ampulheta, um frasco que esvazia, um carrinho a caminho de casa, ou uma imagem que aparece aos bocadinhos. As rotinas da manhã, da hora de dormir e de arrumar fazem-se um passo de cada vez, cada um com o seu temporizador e a sua imagem, e os pais podem usar fotos da escova de dentes ou da mochila verdadeiras para quem ainda não sabe ler. Como num relógio de cuco, quando o tempo acaba um presente abana e sai um de dezasseis animais; quando os encontram todos, aparece o Cuco, o cuco. As crianças ganham estrelas e medalhas, cada uma tem o seu perfil, e com o iCloud tudo é igual na Apple TV, no iPhone e no iPad. Não tem anúncios, rastreamento nem conta.

What’s special

  • Made for the TVCuco is built for the shared screen in the living room, runs from the Siri Remote, and starts a routine straight from the Top Shelf.
  • Readable before readingFive ways to see time, and photos of a child’s own things for each step, so a routine makes sense to children who can’t read words or numbers.
  • A reason to waitThe surprise animal at the end, the stars, the album and the badges turn waiting and routines into something children look forward to. Everything a child earns is free.
  • Safe for small handsMenu on the remote asks before stopping a timer, iPhone and iPad can lock everything but Done!, and buying is behind a check that a grown-up is there.
